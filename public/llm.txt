# ul0.site — Cheapest Custom Domain Link Shortener & Multi-Tool Platform

ul0 is a fast, minimal, and budget-friendly SaaS platform combining custom domain short links, click tracking, dynamic QR codes, a PDF document scanner, and bill splitting tools.

## 1. Core Services

### Custom Domain Shortener & Branded Links
- Connect custom domains (e.g. link.yourbrand.com).
- Shorten links with custom slug aliases (e.g. link.brand.com/offer).
- Click tracking analytics (geographic distribution, referrer sources, device browser tracking).
- Unlimited public URL shortening via the default `ul0.site` domain (no signup required).

### PDF Scanner & Document Tool (/pdf)
- Fully client-side camera scanning with real-time browser permission checks.
- Native OS camera application capture fallback support (`capture="environment"`).
- Image filters (color, grayscale, black & white), rotation, and drag-and-drop page sorting.
- Merging and creating PDFs client-side via `jspdf` and `pdf-lib`.

### Split Expenses & UPI QR Codes (/split)
- Group bill-splitting algorithm designed to minimize total transactions.
- Auto-generation of UPI payment QR codes for instant scans.
- Shareable temporary split session links.
